Project Overview

Healthcare Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end framework that turns 54,860 raw patient-admission records into executive, operational, financial, and predictive intelligence — built to show how a hospital system decides, not just what a dataset contains.

54,860Admissions analyzed
$1.40BTotal billing
8Analytics modules
6ML models compared

Business Problem

Hospital leadership holds large volumes of operational and patient data but lacks one consolidated view of volume, performance, revenue, insurance patterns, doctor workload, disease trends, and resource demand — so decisions get made on fragments rather than the whole system.

Business Objective

Give management a single decision-support layer spanning descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics — moving from Data → Chart → Model Accuracy to Data → Insight → Prediction → Decision.

Stakeholders

  • CEO / Hospital Director — executive oversight
  • Operations Manager — hospital benchmarking
  • Finance / Revenue Management — cost & billing
  • Insurance / Revenue Cycle — payer analysis
  • Medical Director — doctor workload
  • Clinical Management — patient population
  • Capacity Planning — resource demand

Key Questions

  • What is happening across the system right now?
  • Where are the operational and financial pressure points?
  • What is driving cost, capacity, and workload patterns?
  • What could happen, and what should management do next?

Expected Impact

A reusable decision-support layer — not a one-time report — that replaces fragmented spreadsheet views with a governed fact/dimension model feeding both executive dashboards and a predictive-analytics prototype.
